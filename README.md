# Luis Says Hi 👋

📍 **Camarines Sur, Philippines** | 🤖 **Senior Software Engineer** | 🎓 **MBA Candidate**

Luis is a language-agnostic engineer who picks the right tool for the job. He has spent his career working with small startup teams across insurtech, cybersecurity compliance, social-media automation, and Southeast Asian job markets. He started at FastJobs in 2019 as a fullstack developer and grew into a senior role leading a 5-person team, shipping a job auto-refresh microservice for 100K+ daily users across 3 markets. He built ApptegaGPT (OpenAI-powered cybersecurity compliance recommendations) at Apptega, owned email reliability and policy-data integrity across microservices at Arch/Thimble (50K+ policyholders), and led the AI agents team at Parallel Distribution building TikTok scraping and account-ban reduction systems. Today he is focused on AI-augmented engineering, contributing to open source, and pursuing an MBA to better connect technology with business strategy.

## What Luis is Up To

- Exploring IoT as a hobby
